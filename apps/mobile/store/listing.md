## Full description (max 4000 chars)

```
TideChartsPro gives you accurate, easy-to-read tide charts and tide times for thousands of coastal stations across 23 U.S. states - all powered by live data from NOAA. Whether you fish, boat, surf, paddle, dig clams, or just walk the beach, you'll always know exactly when the tide is high, low, and turning.

No account required. No clutter. Just fast, reliable tide data.

KEY FEATURES

Interactive tide charts
See the full day's tide curve at a glance, with high and low tide pins, exact times, and heights. The chart shows negative low tides so you can plan around the lowest water.

Today's tide times
High tide and low tide times and heights for the day, updated from NOAA tide predictions.

Water temperature
Live water temperature from the nearest NOAA station - useful for fishing, surfing, and swimming.

Hourly weather forecast
Next-24-hour and daily weather from the NOAA National Weather Service: temperature, wind, and precipitation, right alongside your tides.

Solunar fishing forecast
Major and minor solunar periods for each day, plus a simple fishing-quality rating, so you can time your trips to peak feeding activity.

Tide & moon calendar
A monthly calendar view with tides, moon phase, sunrise, tidal coefficient, and the day's fishing outlook - perfect for planning trips ahead.

Find stations fast
Browse tide stations by state on an interactive map, search by name, or tap "nearby" to find the closest stations to your location using GPS.

WHY TIDECHARTSPRO

- Live NOAA data: tide predictions, water levels, and water temperature come straight from NOAA CO-OPS, with weather from the National Weather Service.
- Built for the coast: thousands of stations covering the Atlantic, Gulf, and Pacific coasts across 23 states.
- Privacy first: no sign-up, no ads tracking you, no selling your data. Location is used only on your device to find nearby stations and is never uploaded.
- Clean and fast: a simple, modern interface that loads quickly and works the way you expect.

PERFECT FOR

- Anglers timing trips around tides and solunar periods
- Boaters and kayakers planning launches and returns
- Surfers and paddlers checking conditions and water temp
- Clammers and beachcombers chasing the lowest low tides
- Anyone who lives near or visits the coast

Download TideChartsPro and never get caught out by the tide again.

Tide and water data provided by NOAA. TideChartsPro is not affiliated with NOAA.
```
